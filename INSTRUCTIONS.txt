================================================================================
DARK BIOLINK & DEVELOPER PORTFOLIO TEMPLATE
================================================================================

Simple setup instructions for your biolink website.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. MEDIA FILES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Drop your files into this folder:

  avatar.jpg     -> Your profile avatar image.
  background.mp4 -> Looping background video (optional).
  audio.mp3      -> Background music track (optional).

If you don't add background.mp4 or audio.mp3, the site works automatically
with a clean dark obsidian background.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. CUSTOMIZING YOUR INFO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Open "config.js" in any text editor (Notepad, VS Code, etc.):
  - name: your username
  - bio: phrases for the terminal typewriter
  - discord: your discord handle
  - github: your github url
  - ltc: your litecoin address
  - audio: track title and artist

Open "index.html" to paste your project links and descriptions:
  Find "<!-- project 1 -->" and paste your link into <a href="...">.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. RUN LOCALLY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Double click "index.html" to open in your browser.
Or with python: python -m http.server 3000

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. FREE GITHUB PAGES HOSTING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Create a new repository on https://github.com/new
2. Run "push_to_github.bat" in this folder and paste your repository URL.
3. In your repo, go to Settings -> Pages -> choose "main" branch and save.
4. Your website is live at https://<username>.github.io/<repo>/
